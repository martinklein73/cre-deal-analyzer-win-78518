## About CRE Deal Analyzer

CRE Deal Analyzer is a browser-based calculator for commercial real estate underwriting and deal analysis. It brings core evaluation workflows into one HTML-focused application, helping users review assumptions, compare outcomes, and examine how changing inputs may affect a deal.

The tool is designed for people working with commercial real estate scenarios, including analysts, investors, and other users who need a structured way to assess potential transactions. Saved deals, member access, usage tracking, and an AI assistant chat provide supporting features around the primary underwriting workflow.
